Growing responsibly

Good food should be good for the future.

Sustainability is not an add-on at Nabatna. It shapes who we buy from, the technologies we back and the way we serve our customers.

01

Sustainable vegetation & production

We prioritise produce grown with respect for land and water, and we work with suppliers who share that approach. Fewer wasted resources on the farm means better food on your plate.

  • Preference for responsibly grown produce
  • Efficient sourcing to reduce waste along the chain
  • Long-term relationships with growers who farm sustainably
02

Supporting local farmers

Buying local keeps value in the community, shortens the distance from farm to kitchen and delivers fresher produce. We actively look to UAE growers first and work alongside them to bring their harvests to Dubai's chefs.

  • Local produce prioritised whenever quality and availability allow
  • A dependable route to market for UAE growers
  • Shorter supply chains and fresher deliveries
03

Backing vertical farming technology

In a desert climate, growing up instead of out makes sense. Vertical farms grow crops in stacked, controlled-environment layers, using far less land and water and producing right here in the UAE, all year round.

  • Support for vertical farms as a growing source of local produce
  • Year-round, consistent supply independent of the weather
  • Fresh greens and herbs grown close to Dubai's kitchens
04

Championing agro-tech

Smarter farming means less waste and more reliable harvests. We keep a close eye on agricultural technology and support the innovators using it to make local growing more efficient and resilient.

  • Hydroponics, controlled-environment and precision agriculture
  • Data-driven growing that saves water and energy
  • Partnerships with agri-tech growers bringing new ideas to market
